# Reuse and attribution

The public Drive Helper is free to use. This source publication preserves existing permissions and adds no general-purpose reuse licence for the original Weidu code or branding. Do not describe the whole repository as MIT-licensed.

The MIT licence in `NOBLE-HASHES-LICENSE.txt` applies to the separately identified @noble/hashes component. Its authors and permission notice remain intact. Build dependencies keep their own licences.

Questions about broader reuse can be raised through [Weidu Fanling](https://weidufanling.au/). Publishing a patch does not authorise changing the live website.
